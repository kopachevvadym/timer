Privacy policy

Updated October 26, 2026. Service: timer.kopachev.life — time tracking for AI agents.

In short

  • We store only what time tracking needs: project and task names, working time, and your sign-in profile.
  • Your code, file contents and full prompts are not stored.
  • Only you can see your data. We don’t sell it, share it or show ads.
  • You can delete your account with all data at any time in Settings — immediately and permanently.

What we store

  • Sign-in profile. GitHub: numeric id, login, name, avatar. Google: the permanent account identifier (sub), email, name, profile picture. Also your time zone and preferences.
  • Time tracking. Projects (name, folder path on your computer, git repository), tasks (title, notes, issue number), time entries (start, end, agent session identifier).
  • The start of the prompt (up to 140 characters) — only if you turn this on in Settings yourself. Off by default.
  • API keys and access tokens — only as hashes (SHA-256); we don’t store the keys themselves.

Google data

Through Google sign-in we receive only the openid, email and profile permissions and use them solely to recognize you at sign-in and to show your name and email in Settings. We have no access to your mail, files or other Google services. Use of data received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies

Only necessary ones: a session cookie (to keep you signed in), a temporary cookie during sign-in (protection against request forgery), and a cookie with the interface language. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Where data is stored

The service runs on Cloudflare infrastructure (Workers, D1, KV). Cloudflare processes data as the hosting provider. There are no other recipients.

Deletion and your rights

Settings → Danger zone → Delete account permanently deletes your profile, projects, tasks, entries and keys. You can unlink GitHub or Google there as well. You can view and change your data in the interface at any time.

Contact

Questions about your data — via github.com/kopachevvadym/timer/issues.
